    Teach the library what you mean

    Pick a tag — one you already use or a new one — and the model learns it from the music itself: key and scale, chords, timbre, groove, how the track breathes, plus your own tags and genres.

    1. Answer — yes or no on the tracks it is least sure about. Keys: Y N, S to skip, U to undo, Space to listen, T to tag it or file it in a playlist.
    2. Model — how often it is right on tracks it was never shown, and which kind of information it is listening to.
    3. Results — the whole library ranked. Correct it with ✓ and ✗ (that trains it too) and tag what belongs.
